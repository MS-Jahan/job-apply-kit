Short LinkedIn search queries find fresh posts; long ones return nothing

Software Developer AND (Hiring OR Vacancy)
AND (Dhaka OR Bangladesh)
  1. Open LinkedIn search, choose Posts
  2. Sort by Latest
  3. Remote? Add (Remote OR Hybrid)
WarningLinkedIn has a length limit. Do not chain every query with AND. Keep several short ones.
TipUpload your CV to any chat model and ask it to write queries for your roles.
These AND/OR queries are sometimes called dorks: a short search query with boolean operators. Posts from a few hours ago show up, so the freshness is real. A queries list sits in your config under linkedin_queries.

OAuth client means: a sign-in key your own computer uses to talk to your own Google account. The four scopes are gmail.readonly, gmail.compose, drive and spreadsheets. Four Google gotchas cause most setup failures

🔒 Never share the JSON

The client file and tokens are password-equivalent. Never commit them.

⏳ 7 days unused

Testing-mode tokens expire. Sign in again; daily use keeps it alive.

🔗 IDs are in URLs

Folder: /folders/<ID>. Sheet: /d/<ID>/edit.

✨ Missing folder or Sheet?

The agent offers to create them (sheet_init.py).

Moving to production needs Google verification and is out of scope for personal use. Stay in Testing with yourself as the test user.
